# ADR-005: Pseudonymous visitor identity

Status: accepted (brownfield scenario)

## Decision

visitor_id = first 16 hex chars of SHA-256(secret salt | IP | user-agent); referrer stored as host only.

## Rationale

Legal requires no raw IPs; unique-visitor counts need a stable id. A secret salt prevents reversing the 2^32 IPv4 space by brute force.

## Alternatives considered

- Store IPs encrypted (still personal data, key management burden)
- Drop unique visitors (loses a requested metric)
- Cookies (changes client behaviour, consent implications)
