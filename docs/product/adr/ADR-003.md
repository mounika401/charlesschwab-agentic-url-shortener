# ADR-003: 307 Temporary Redirect

Status: accepted (greenfield scenario)

## Decision

Redirect with 307 as chosen by the requester at clarification.

## Rationale

Permanent redirects are cached by browsers, so later clicks would bypass the service: destinations could not change and click counts would be wrong.

## Alternatives considered

- 301/308 permanent (cacheable, breaks counting)
- 302 (method may change to GET on POST)
